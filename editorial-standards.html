Our Standards

Editorial standards

How we research, review, and update every guide on TrustSmartSaving.

Independence

TrustSmartSaving operates independently of any single advertiser or product provider. Our editorial decisions — what we cover, how we cover it, and what we recommend — are made by our editorial team, not by anyone selling a product. We do not accept payment in exchange for coverage, and we do not write sponsored guides.

Research methodology

Every guide on TrustSmartSaving starts with research. We pull pricing data from publicly available sources, review current consumer protection guidance from federal and state agencies, study cost surveys and industry reports, and synthesize what we find into plain-English breakdowns. Where a guide references a specific dollar figure or statistic, that figure reflects the most recent data we could verify at the time of publication or last update.

Review process

Before publication, every guide is reviewed by a second member of our editorial team. The reviewer checks every cited number, verifies that the guidance is consistent with current consumer protection rules, and confirms that the writing is clear and accurate. Guides that involve regulated topics — insurance, credit, debt — get an additional pass to confirm we are not making promises we cannot back up.

Updates and corrections

The world changes. Insurance rules shift. Average remodel costs creep up. Credit reporting rules get amended. We track those changes and update our guides as needed. Every guide has a "Published" date and an "Updated" date so you can see when it was last touched. If we get something wrong, we correct it and note the correction at the bottom of the guide.

Conflicts of interest

TrustSmartSaving may, in the future, work with financial service providers, lenders, or contractors who want to reach our audience. Any such relationship is disclosed clearly on the page where it appears, and never influences our editorial guides. Our guides remain free of sponsored product placements.

Have a question?

If you spot an error, have a question about a guide, or want to suggest a topic, contact us at editorial@trustsmartsaving.com.
