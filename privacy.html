Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TrustSmartSaving ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit trustsmartsaving.com (the "Site").

Information we collect

We collect very limited information from visitors to this Site.

When you submit our contact form, we receive the information you provide — typically your name, email address, and the message you send us. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry.

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may automatically log basic technical information about visits to the Site, such as IP addresses and browser type, for security and infrastructure purposes. We do not use this information for marketing or profiling.

What we do not collect

We want to be specific about what we do not do, because many websites do these things by default and we have chosen not to:

  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this Site.
  • We do not run Google Analytics or similar visitor analytics.
  • We do not run remarketing pixels (Meta Pixel, Google Ads, TikTok Pixel, etc.).
  • We do not collect email addresses for newsletter or marketing lists.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share visitor data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

This Site does not set tracking cookies. Cloudflare may use minimal session cookies for security and bot-protection purposes; these are not used for advertising or analytics.

Contact form submissions

Information submitted through our contact form is reviewed by our editorial team and used only to respond to you. We retain contact form submissions for a reasonable period for record-keeping. If you would like us to delete a message you have sent us, contact editorial@trustsmartsaving.com.

Children's privacy

This Site is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact editorial@trustsmartsaving.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Residents of California may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. Residents of the European Economic Area may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation. We honor applicable requests from residents of any jurisdiction whose laws grant them such rights.

Links to other sites

This Site may include links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our handling of your information, contact us at editorial@trustsmartsaving.com.
